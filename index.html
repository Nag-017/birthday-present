On This Day in History

NOTABLE EVENTS ON FRIDAY, OCTOBER 20 ACROSS THE YEARS

★ FROM THE ARCHIVES
  • •

    2006 — On the exact Friday Megha arrived, Diwali celebrations began across India as Shah Rukh Khan's musical thriller Don (featuring Shankar-Ehsaan-Loy's "Aaj Ki Raat") and the romantic musical Jaan-E-Mann (with Sonu Nigam's "Sau Dard Hain") were officially released in cinemas nationwide.

  • •

    2006 — Karnataka's music industry experienced an unprecedented golden wave: the hit romance Jothe Jotheyali completed silver-jubilee runs driven by "O Gunavantha" (Sonu Nigam & Shreya Ghoshal), while recording concluded on Mungaru Male—the Mano Murthy and Jayanth Kaikini soundtrack that set all-time audio sales records across Karnataka.

  • •

    1995 — Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge (DDLJ) premiered in Indian cinemas on October 20. Its iconic soundtrack composed by Jatin-Lalit ("Tujhe Dekha Toh Yeh Jaana Sanam", "Mehndi Laga Ke Rakhna") permanently transformed Indian pop music and romance, becoming the longest-running film in Indian cinema history.

  • •

    The Vampire Diaries Lore — For fans of Mystic Falls, October 20 holds special television lore: the acclaimed Season 3 episode "Smells Like Teen Spirit" originally premiered on October 20, 2011, while in show canon, mid-to-late October marks the legendary Night of the Comet—the fateful night Damon Salvatore first swept back into town (and fellow October Libra Caroline Forbes was born just ten days prior on October 10!).

  • •

    Karnataka Heritage — October 20 marks the auspicious period of Mahanavami & Ayudha Puja during the world-renowned Mysuru Dasara (Nada Habba), filling Karnataka with Carnatic classical concerts, devotional Dasara Pada, and grand palace festivities celebrating the state's artistic heritage.
